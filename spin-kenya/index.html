About the club

Spin Kenya is a table tennis club built around discipline on the table and community off it. We coach players from first serve to national competition.

Vision

To make Kenya a recognised force in African table tennis — a country that consistently produces players who compete, and win, on continental and world stages.

Mission

We develop athletes through structured coaching, regular league play and mentorship, while making the sport accessible to new players across Kenyan communities.
